Turn your denial letter into an appeal

Upload a photo or PDF of the letter your insurer sent you. We'll explain what it says, ask you a few questions, and draft an appeal letter you can review, edit, and send yourself.

Not ready to upload a real letter? Try it with a sample denial letter — nothing is uploaded, this just shows you how it works.

Before you upload

  • This currently handles commercial health plans — employer, marketplace, or individual coverage. Medicare and Medicaid appeals follow a different process we don't support yet.
  • Your document is read once to pull out the facts, then deleted. We don't keep it.
  • This works best with the actual denial letter (sometimes called a "notice of adverse benefit determination"), not just the Explanation of Benefits.
