This tool explains denial letters and helps you draft your own appeal. It is not legal advice or medical advice, and it does not send anything on your behalf — you review and send the letter yourself.

We don't keep your uploaded document. It's deleted immediately after we read it, and nothing about your case is stored beyond this browser session unless you ask us to set a deadline reminder.
